# Enhancement roadmap (not included in prototype)

## Boss Telegraphs
- A player-friendly timer/bar and directional templates tied to actual attack items
- Optional PF2e saving throw / damage resolution with GM confirmation

## Boss Phase Director
- Metamorph integration if enabled, audio scenes, ability loadouts, spawned adds and hazard templates

## Dynamic Battlefield
- Saved presets, template collision/containment checks, condition-safe GM-approved effects, hazard disarming

## Weak Points
- Component-targeting context menus, targeted roll resolution, per-part resistances/hardness, disable ability links

## Horde Commander
- Orders/formation handling, squad morale, shared initiative (opt in), PF2e group attack rules with system-safe calls

## Break Gauge
- Per-damage-type buildup rules, optional PF2e statuses, automatic recovery and stagger duration

## Cinematic Dialogue
- Portrait editor, sound playback, expressions, event triggers, branching responses

## Environmental Interactions
- Real on-tile prompt and click interaction, validated PF2e skill rolls, map effects

## Monster Behavior
- Ally-death and leader-loss cues, scripted surrender/retreat phases; no player-facing secret leaks

## Encounter Objectives
- Combatant-driven objectives, zone capture and puzzle progress, reusable templates, secrecy controls
